# Hacking Game (CE002)

Find each hacking target on the event field, then win a short top-down shooter stage by destroying the target once its guards are gone.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/ce002.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

- Intro story: "Suspicious Device" (`d_contents_ce002_mini_01`)
- Art folder: `icons-event/ce002`

## Overview

### How it works

- **Orders**: Accept one hacking data order at a time; orders unlock in sequence over the event.
- **Finding the target**: The order gives a location hint; walk there on the event field and press Hacking.
- **Controls**: Touch and drag to move. Your unit fires on its own, at nearby enemies or in the direction it moves.
- **Winning**: Destroy the target. While any other enemy is alive, the target has a shield.
- **Losing**: Getting hit 3 times (300 HP, 100 damage per enemy bullet) or running out of time.
- **After clearing**: The stage can be replayed, and its archive entry can be read from the menu.
- **Intro story**: story "Suspicious Device" (`d_contents_ce002_mini_01`)

## Tutorial

### Step 1

- ■ You may accept the order after confirming the hacking target's information. / ■ Only one set of hacking data can be received each time. / ■ The hacking data will be revealed in sequence.

### Step 2

- ■ Look for the hacking target in the event field. / ■ Hints about the target's location will be provided. / ■ Enter the hacking game by clicking the Hacking button.

### Step 3

- ■ Touch and drag on the screen to move the player unit. / ■ Projectiles can be used to attack and kill enemies. / ■ Victory is achieved upon the elimination of the target. / ■ The game is over when player unit is attacked 3 times or when time is up.

### Step 4

- ■ Cleared stage data will be saved in the Mini Game. / ■ You can view the archive or replay the stage.
