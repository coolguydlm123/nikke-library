# Mini Game (CE009)

An endless maze run: swipe through the corridors collecting coins and items, dodge Shadows and outrun the collapsing map while your HP drains.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/ce009.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

## Overview

### How it plays

- **Moving**: Swipe (or WASD / arrow keys) to pick a direction; your character runs until she hits a wall, then waits for the next input.
- **HP**: You start with 900 HP. It drains steadily (faster at higher difficulty), and each enemy you run into takes 300 HP (the enemy is destroyed). After a hit you are invincible for 1 s.
- **Collapse**: After a while (start delay 5 s) the map collapses from behind and follows you; touching it ends the run, as does reaching 0 HP.
- **Score**: 200 points per metre, 750 per enemy defeated with an item or skill, coin points and combo bonuses.
- **Ranks**: B 100,000, A 150,000, S 200,000 points
- **Persona skills**: Coins and treasure chests fill a skill gauge; when it is full the character’s Persona skill fires by itself.
- **Daily reward**: Score 30,000 in one run: Gem ×30
- **Intro story**: story "Phantom Thief Simulator" (`d_contents_ce009_mini_01`)
