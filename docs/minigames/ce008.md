# Lyco Reco Beats (CE008)

A one-button rhythm game: tap on the beat to dodge, shoot and decorate desserts. Clear stages for stamps that level up talents, then take on Hard stages with score-boosting mods.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/ce008.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

## Overview

### How to play

- **Controls**: Tap anywhere on the screen at the correct timing. (When playing on PC, use the spacebar.)
- **Life**: 40 life (4 misses); each miss costs 10. Every 10 combo restores 5. At 0 the stage fails.
- **Points**: Perfect 350, Great 250, Good 150, Miss 0 on the first stage; some stages pay less (see Timing windows)
- **Stars**: Each stage has three star conditions (see Stages).
- **Stages**: Clearing stages on Normal after the specified date will unlock Hard Mode.
- **Talents**: Limited Dessert Stamps are awarded each time you clear a stage on Normal for the first time. Coffee Stamps are awarded each time you clear a stage.
- **Union Scores**: Your total score from either Normal Mode stages or Hard Mode stages will be recorded, whichever is higher.
- **Daily reward**: Play once a day: Gem ×30

### Hard Mode mods

| Mod | Effect | Score bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard Rock** | Increases the level of accuracy required. | +10% |
| **Double Time** | Doubles the amount of life lost. | +10% |
| **Sudden Death** | Ends the game if you miss. | +20% |
| **Perfection** | Counts "good" and "great" attempts as misses. | +15% |
| **Flashlight** | Applies a blur effect to the screen. | +15% |

Mods can be switched on for Hard stages; each adds its bonus to the points you score.

## Game guide

### Lyco Reco Beats

- This is a rhythm game. Please ensure background music is turned on when playing. / Settings→ Audio→ Background Music
- Each stage has a specific gameplay style. Please read through the description of each stage.
- Tap anywhere on the screen at the correct timing. (When playing on PC, use the spacebar.)

### Dodge training

- Control Chisato to practice dodging. Pay attention to the type of weapon Takina is using, then tap the screen at the correct timing to dodge. (When playing on PC, use the spacebar.)
- The pistol is loaded with a single training bullet, meaning you'll only need to dodge once.
- When Takina is using the pistol, dodge at the moment she shoots.
- The shotgun is loaded with two training bullets, meaning you'll need to dodge twice.
- The shotgun needs to be reloaded. Dodge after the rhythm point.
- The machine gun is loaded with three training bullets, meaning you'll need to dodge three times consecutively.
- The machine gun needs to be reloaded. The first bullet requires a delayed dodge, after which you must dodge sequentially in sync with the rhythm.
- Takina will fire at set intervals. Make sure to dodge three times in total.

### Rhythmic shooting

- Practice shooting in this VR training. Remember the order in which the monsters appear, then tap the screen at the correct timing to shoot them in order. (When playing on PC, use the spacebar.)
- Monsters: In the VR game, monsters will appear with specific rhythms. Take them down by shooting to the rhythm.

### Dessert making

- Control Chisato to make dessert. Pay attention to the state of the dessert, then tap the screen at the correct timing to finish making it. (When playing on PC, use the spacebar.)
- When a dessert base arrives in front of Chisato, tap the screen and hold to spread chocolate, then release when finished.
- When a chocolate-frosted dessert arrives in front of Chisato, tap the screen and hold to add sauce, then release when finished.

### Talents

- You can use Coffee Stamps and Limited Dessert Stamps to level up different talents that will make the game easier.
- Limited Dessert Stamps are awarded each time you clear a stage on Normal for the first time.
- Coffee Stamps are awarded each time you clear a stage.

### Changing the Difficulty

- Modifiers (Mods) are a feature in Hard Mode that you can enable.
- Selecting a mod allows you to try your hand at a harder difficulty.
- In addition to increasing the difficulty, mods also increase the points you get for clearing a stage.

### Union Scores

- The Union Scores feature allows you to share your high score with your fellow Union members.
- Your total score from either Normal Mode stages or Hard Mode stages will be recorded, whichever is higher.
- After you join a Union, your score will be recorded in Union Scores when you clear a stage.
