# Defense Game (CE004)

Read the Rapture’s hints and answer each with the right attack or defence button; correct answers deal damage, block attacks and charge a Burst Skill.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/ce004.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

## Overview

### How it plays

- **Turns**: Each turn the Rapture shows one to three hints. Pick the matching buttons before time runs out. Attack hints (core or a side part exposed) let you hit it; defence hints (missiles, beams) must be blocked or you take damage.
- **Buttons**: Left Part Attack; [Attack]; Core Attack; [Attack]; Right Part Attack; [Attack]; Left Beam Interruption; [Defense]; Aiming Projectile; [Defense]; Right Beam Interruption; [Defense]
- **Difficulty**: Easy Raptures only expose their core or fire missiles; Normal adds the left and right parts; Hard adds left and right beams and up to three actions per turn.
- **Burst**: Every right answer fills the Burst Gauge (1,000, or 1,500 for a PERFECT, out of 25,000). When it is full, the character’s Burst Skill deals heavy damage.
- **Charge bonus**: Each successful defence adds a charge that boosts your next attack (+10% per charge); a wrong answer resets it.
- **Winning**: Bring the Rapture’s HP to 0 before yours runs out. Clearing an Easy, Normal or Hard stage gives Trait Points.
- **Daily reward**: Deal 500 damage in a day: Gem ×30
- **Challenge**: An endless run against ever stronger Raptures; your highest damage counts for the Union ranking.
- **Intro story**: story "Battle Simulator" (`d_contents_ce004_mini_01`)
