# Merch Bag

Scratch lottery tickets to collect T.T. STAR merch, then decorate a merch bag with it. Collecting more designs pays rewards; playing the lottery more unlocks new bags and ribbons.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/staranis.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

### What is a merch bag?

- Collect T.T. STAR merch to decorate your merch bag.
- You can obtain merch through events and use the Decorate option to decorate your bag.
- Put together a merch bag for your favorite group member.
- You can acquire scratch tickets by opening up the merch bag page once a day, and by finding lost relics out in the field.
- Scratch tickets can be used to get merch.
- Collect merch to earn rewards.

### The lottery

- **Ticket**: 1 T.T. STAR Scratch Ticket per play
- **Each play**: Opens a pack of merch (pack size 10; meaning not fully confirmed)
- **Box**: 1,374 pieces of merch in 54 designs; what you draw leaves the box
- **Daily gift**: 5 tickets a day for opening the merch bag page, 10 from 2026-05-06 20:00
- **Other tickets**: Found as lost relics in the event field
- **Unlocks**: Opens after clearing an event field stage (110810105)
