# In The Mirror

In The Mirror: explore a side-scrolling mirror world as Cinderella, defeat Raptures for Gold and Cores, unlock new Glass Slippers abilities through main missions, and replay it on NEW GAME+. Also includes the April Fools Mecha Shifty stage built on the same engine.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/oldtales.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 4 rows.

- Art folder: `icons-event/48_oldtales`

## Overview

### How it plays

Cinderella explores 8 connected chapters (plus a hidden “???” danger zone) by running, jumping and shooting with her Glass Slippers. Main missions lead from area to area; each one points you to a spot, a boss or an item, and many of them unlock a new ability (Dash, Double Jump, Absorb Mode, Glass Slippers B/C/D, Burst).

Defeated Raptures drop Gold (and, from bosses on NEW GAME+, Cores). Spend them in the Upgrade Shop on six permanent upgrades, and in the General Shop on Modules and Memories. Modules are equipment that tweak a skill; each costs module energy and you can only equip as much as your energy allows.

Hidden around the map are 40 Memories (lore texts) and 16 Keys, which open locked doors. Touching a save point (a petal-shaped glass fragment) saves everything; there is only one save file.

After the ending you can start NEW GAME+: the difficulty rises, exploration, upgrades, modules and items are kept, and the main missions start over (with a repeatable boss-hunt mission and bigger drops).

### Rules of the world

- **Dropped items**: Disappear after 180 s if not picked up
- **Changing rooms**: 2 s of invincibility on entering a room
- **Hazards and objects**: Electric and fire traps, moving platforms, floor switches, locked doors and item boxes (some need a key)
- **Easy Mode**: An option for story viewing: Cinderella’s HP does not go down
- **NEW GAME+**: 20 difficulty steps after the first game
