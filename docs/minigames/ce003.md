# Serving Game (CE003)

Run the Re:Zero maid cafe for 90 seconds: cook what customers order, have Emilia, Rem or Ram carry it to the right table before they give up, and clean up after them while watching the maids’ HP. Event customers start a ketchup timing game.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/ce003.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 30 rows.

## Overview

### A shift

- **Intro story**: story "Re:ADY TO ORDER?" (`d_contents_ce003_mini_01`)
- **Time limit**: 90 s (warning 10 s before the end)
- **Kitchen**: 4 regular dishes, each ordered equally often; each takes 2 s to cook. 1 cooking slot at first; extra orders wait in the production queue.
- **Event dish**: Only event customers order it; serving it starts the ketchup game
- **Serving**: A maid carries the dish to the table; a dish whose customer has left can be thrown away
- **Unserved**: A customer who waits too long leaves and Satisfaction is deducted
- **Cleaning**: After eating, a table may need cleaning, which costs HP but earns Satisfaction
- **Daily reward**: 500 Satisfaction in a day: Gem ×30
