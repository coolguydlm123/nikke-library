# Memory of Goddess (NKS)

MEMORY OF GODDESS: a survivor-style shooter. Drag a Goddess Squad member around while Raptures swarm in, level up with Battle Data to collect weapons and modules, outlast the stage timer and kill the boss.

This guide was generated from the Nikke Library's reading of the game data: the viewer `lib/eventgames/redash.js` turns the `Event*` tables in `D:\Nikke Datamine\mpkparser\jsons` into the rules below. Numbers, units and formulas are the viewer's readings; anything it was not sure of is listed under "Uncertain / unknown". Long lists are cut to 4 rows.

- Art folder: `icons-event/24_redash`

## Overview

### How a run works

- **Goal**: Survive until the stage timer runs out (5 to 30 minutes) and defeat the boss. The timer stops while a mid-boss is on the field.
- **Controls**: Tap and drag to move; your weapons fire on their own. Double-tap the character (or the Burst button) for her Burst.
- **Losing**: The run ends when HP hits 0 (EX Edition: revive with Coins if you have revivals left).
- **Levelling up**: Battle Data dropped by Raptures is EXP. Each level offers a random choice of weapons and modules; picking one you own levels it up (Lv. 5 is max). Level 1 needs 14 EXP, level 10 needs 42, and every level from 20 on needs 166.
- **Mid-bosses**: Tougher Raptures appear at fixed times; they drop a Manufacturer Arms box with free weapon / module upgrades.
- **After a hit**: You are invincible for 0.5 s.
- **Between runs**: Coins picked up in runs carry over: spend them on each character’s skill tree (refundable) and, in EX, on character unlocks and the Coin upgrade store.
- **Relics**: Some stages hide a relic on the map. Relics unlock characters and, carried by the right character to the end of the right stage, play her ending story.

### Versions

- **M.O.G.**: The original event: 5 themes of 2 stages plus a 30-minute Challenge Stage, 6 characters.; story "Battle Simulation?" (`1ann_mini_prologue`)
- **M.O.G. EX Edition**: The Arcade version: adds Exia, the Mine theme, a “Ø” stage per theme, Challenge Stages α and β, EX. combined weapons, Coin upgrades, revives and rerolls.; story "The Legendary Game" (`mog2_prologue`)
- **Union Ranking**: Ordinary Raptures killed in the Challenge Stage (Challenge Stage - β in EX) count for a ranking inside your Union. Not counted in the Archives.

## How to play

The in-game guide.

### Memory Of Goddess

- Memory Of Goddess is a gameplay mode / where you destroy Raptures swarming from all directions and survive for a designated period of time.
- **How to Operate**
- Tap and drag to move.
- Use the weapon equipped by the character to attack enemies.
- **Defeat**
- You are defeated when the character's HP is depleted.

### Level Up

- Obtain Battle Data to level up.
- When leveling up, you can choose an item from the randomly designated items. / If you choose an item you own, the item will level up.
- The higher the level, the more Battle Data needed.

### Special Item

- You might obtain items with special effects when killing Raptures.
- **Manufacturer Arms**
- Some Raptures drop Manufacturer Arms when destroyed.
- Obtain Manufacturer Arms to get various types of equipment.
- **Data Collector**
- Attracts all Coins and Battle Data scattered around the field to your location.
- **Lilith Dive**
- Kills all ordinary Raptures on the field.
- **Burst Charger**
- Restores a certain amount of the character's Burst Skill gauge.
- **Emergency Recovery**
- Restores a certain amount of the character's HP.

### Burst Skill

- Tap the character twice or tap the Burst button / to use the character's powerful skill.
- Every character has her own Burst Skill.
- **Burst Cooldown**
- After the Burst Skill is used, it cannot be used again for a certain period of time.
- **Configuring the Usage Method of Burst Skills**
- You can configure the usage method of Burst Skills in Settings.

### Unlock Character

- Fulfill specific conditions to unlock other characters.
- **Unlock Conditions**
- Clear specific stages.
- Obtain special items in the stages.
- Fulfill the two conditions above to purchase characters using Coins.

### Skill Upgrade

- Use the Coins you obtained to upgrade the character's skill.
- **Skill Reset**
- You can reset skills and retrieve all Coins used in upgrading.

### Missions

- Missions you can complete by playing Memory Of Goddess.
- **Challenge Missions**
- You can complete Challenge Missions by playing Memory Of Goddess.

### Save and Quit

- When you return to the Lobby from the pause screen, / you can save the current progress and return to the title screen.
- Only 1 Saved Data slot can be used. The following data will be saved:
- Character Level, EXP, Burst Gauge
- Weapon and Module in use
- Owned items used to unlock characters
- Skill upgrade progress at the time of saving
- Save time
- **Skill**
- Only the skill upgrade progress at the time of saving will be retained.
- **Boss Battle**
- Changes to the situation right before the Boss appears.
- **M.O.G. EX Edition will save the following information:**
- Remaining resurrection attempts
- Remaining upgrade reroll attempts

### Stage

- There are 11 stages in Memory Of Goddess.
- You can only choose Snowy Land - I as the first stage.
- You must clear the previous stage to enter the next stage.
- Rare items may drop in some stages.
- **Stage Clear Conditions**
- The stage is only cleared when you reach the specified  / survival duration and kill the Boss for each theme.
- However, time will not pass when the mid-boss appears.
- **Challenge Stage**
- Unlocked after Snowy Land - II is cleared.
- The number of ordinary Raptures killed in the Challenge stage will be reflected in the Union Ranking.
- Union Ranking is not supported in the Archives. The number of ordinary Raptures defeated in the Challenge Stage will not be individually recorded in the Ranking.

### Union Ranking

- Union Ranking is an internal ranking of your Union / determined by the highest number of Raptures killed / in the Challenge stage of Memory Of Goddess.
- **Ranking Criteria**
- The criteria of the Union Ranking / is the highest number of Raptures killed by the end of the Challenge stage.
- **Ranking Scope**
- Union Ranking only counts the scores of Union members / who have played the Challenge Stage.
- In M.O.G. EX Edition, only the records of Challenge Stage - β will be counted.
